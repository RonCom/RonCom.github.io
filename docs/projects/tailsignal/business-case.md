# Business case

Three five-year cases, each formula-driven with Low, Base and High scenarios. The partner-network case doesn't
break even within five years. The owned-network case pays back in Year 4 (Base) at 190 locations. Buying partner data
on top of the owned network adds $0.42M of Year-5 impact, all of it from drug-safety studies the owned network can't
reach alone. Every price, volume and cost below is a **planning assumption** to be replaced with quotes and company
figures; the scale gates come from TailSignal's own analyses.

## Case 1: a partner network that pays clinics to share data

### Summary

| | Low | Base | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Partner clinics, Year 5 | 900 | 1,500 | 1,950 |
| Revenue, Year 5 | $2.65M | $4.25M | $5.26M |
| EBITDA, Year 5 | −$1.59M | −$0.89M | −$0.42M |
| EBITDA margin, Year 5 | −60% | −21% | −8% |
| Cumulative EBITDA, Years 1–5 | −$6.87M | −$5.14M | −$3.96M |
| First year with positive EBITDA | Not within 5 years | Not within 5 years | Not within 5 years |
| Extra data licenses needed to break even in Year 5 | 17 | 8 | 4 |

Scenarios scale the partner ramp (Low 0.6×, High 1.3×) and every price (Low 0.8×, High 1.15×).

### Base case, year by year

| $ thousands | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3 | Year 4 | Year 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Partner clinics | 25 | 120 | 400 | 900 | 1,500 |
| Embedded analytics: premium scorecards | 18 | 86 | 288 | 648 | 1,080 |
| Research and insights: reports | 80 | 160 | 240 | 320 | 400 |
| Data products: licenses | 0 | 240 | 480 | 720 | 960 |
| Commercial models: scoring/API | 0 | 80 | 240 | 400 | 560 |
| Validation studies, common side effects | 0 | 0 | 0 | 500 | 750 |
| Validation studies, rare side effects | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 500 |
| **Total revenue** | **98** | **566** | **1,248** | **2,588** | **4,250** |
| Partner data incentives | 25 | 120 | 400 | 900 | 1,500 |
| Team (4 → 15 people) | 800 | 1,400 | 2,000 | 2,600 | 3,000 |
| Infrastructure | 61 | 66 | 80 | 105 | 135 |
| Study delivery | 0 | 0 | 0 | 200 | 500 |
| **EBITDA** | **−788** | **−1,020** | **−1,232** | **−1,217** | **−885** |

### What it says

1. **No scenario breaks even within five years.** The first-pass assumptions are not a viable plan, and the model
   shows where to look.
2. **Partner incentives are the largest variable cost.** Each clinic brings about $2,800 of revenue in Year 5 and costs
   $1,000. The incentive has to buy data access cheaply, through free scorecards rather than cash.
3. **Data licenses are the swing line.** Eight more licenses at $120,000 would break even in Year 5. Licenses unlock
   only once privacy suppression falls, at about 100 clinics.
4. **Validation studies arrive late.** Common side-effect studies unlock in Year 4 and rare ones in Year 5 (Base). In the
   Base case they are 29% of Year-5 revenue ($1.25M of $4.25M).

### When each product unlocks

| Capability | Clinics needed | Low | Base | High | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clinic complication, dental and stewardship scorecards | 9 | Year 1 | Year 1 | Year 1 | Works at today's 9 clinics |
| Data licenses (privacy-safe granularity) | 100 | Year 3 | Year 2 | Year 2 | Assumption; 8% of pets suppressed at 9 clinics |
| Common side-effect validation studies | 720 | Year 5 | Year 4 | Year 4 | ~80× today's network (Model A2) |
| Rare side-effect validation studies | 1,170 | Later | Year 5 | Year 4 | ~130× today's network (Model A2) |

## Case 2: an operator that owns its clinics

For a company that owns every location, such as Destination Pet, ownership changes four things: there are no partner
incentives; most value is internal (staffing, cross-selling across services, clinical quality) and shows up as
EBITDA and therefore exit value; external data sales remain possible but scale-gated; and new costs appear for
unifying systems across acquired brands and for consent and privacy work. Company figures that are not public are
placeholders, flagged for confirmation.

### How many locations

| Source | Figure |
|---|---|
| Synchrony press release, January 2024 | "190+ locations" in more than 35 states |
| [Destination Pet locations page](https://www.destinationpet.com/our-locations/), checked 3 October 2026 | "100s of locations nationwide"; 152 listed, 28 with a veterinary name |
| Pet Palace press release, June 2023 | 160+ locations in more than 31 states |
| VitalPet acquisition, March 2020 | 24 veterinary hospitals in seven states |

The case uses 190 locations, 30 of them vet clinics, and 160 resort locations (daycare, boarding, grooming). The first
version used 160 locations and 40 vet clinics and applied daycare labor savings to every location, including vet
clinics. Staffing savings and resort-to-vet cross-sell now come from resort locations only.

### Summary

| | Low | Base | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Internal value, Year 5 (EBITDA) | $2.28M | $4.57M | $6.40M |
| External data revenue, Year 5 | $0.77M | $0.96M | $1.10M |
| Platform cost, Year 5 | $2.81M | $2.81M | $2.81M |
| **Net EBITDA impact, Year 5** | **$0.24M** | **$2.72M** | **$4.69M** |
| Cumulative net impact, Years 1–5 | −$3.81M | $4.73M | $11.53M |
| Enterprise value at 12× Year-5 impact | $2.9M | $32.6M | $56.3M |
| Payback year | Not within 5 years | Year 4 | Year 3 |

Scenarios scale every internal benefit (Low 0.5×, High 1.4×) and every external price (Low 0.8×, High 1.15×).

**If "hundreds" means more.** Holding 30 vet clinics and every other input at Base:

| Locations | Net EBITDA impact, Year 5 | Cumulative, Years 1–5 | Payback |
|---|---|---|---|
| 152 (listed on the website) | $1.70M | $1.73M | Year 4 |
| 190 (Base) | $2.72M | $4.73M | Year 4 |
| 250 | $4.32M | $9.45M | Year 3 |
| 300 | $5.66M | $13.39M | Year 3 |

Each extra resort location adds about $27,000 of Year-5 impact, mostly staffing savings.

## Case 3: owned network plus bought partner data

Destination Pet keeps its own data and also buys de-identified records from independent vet clinics. Partner data
adds no staffing or cross-sell value, which come from operating the locations. It adds veterinary records, which
drug-safety studies need; wider coverage for licenses and reports; and scorecard subscriptions sold back to partners.

The owned network's 30 vet clinics produce about 105,000 dog records a year at full rollout. Common side-effect
studies need 792,000 over four years and rare ones 1.29M, so the owned network reaches neither. About 60 partner
clinics on top of the company's 30 clear the rare-study gate over four years.

| Partner data assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Partner vet clinics supplying data | 0, 40, 80, 100, 100 (Years 1–5) |
| Data fee per partner clinic | $1,000 a year (the partner-network case's incentive) |
| Onboarding per new partner | $2,000 one-time |
| Dog records per partner clinic | 3,500 a year (same placeholder as company clinics) |
| Partners buying the premium scorecard | 40% at $1,800 a year |
| Added data staff | 1 in Years 2–3, 2 in Years 4–5 |
| Licenses, reports, studies | Licensees 0, 2, 4, 5, 7; reports 1, 3, 5, 6, 7; common studies 1–3 a year from Year 3 and rare studies 1–2 from Year 4, each only once its gate is met |

| Base case | Case 2: owned | Case 3: owned + partner data |
|---|---|---|
| Internal value, Year 5 | $4.57M | $4.57M |
| External revenue, Year 5 | $0.96M | $2.44M |
| Platform cost, Year 5 | $2.81M | $3.87M |
| **Net EBITDA impact, Year 5** | **$2.72M** | **$3.14M** |
| Cumulative, Years 1–5 | $4.73M | $4.46M |
| Payback | Year 4 | Year 4 |
| Enterprise value at 12× | $32.6M | $37.7M |
| Common side-effect studies | Not in 5 years | Year 4 |
| Rare side-effect studies | Not in 5 years | Year 5 |

| Case 3 by scenario | Low | Base | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net EBITDA impact, Year 5 | $0.46M | $3.14M | $5.26M |
| Cumulative, Years 1–5 | −$4.40M | $4.46M | $11.51M |
| Payback | Not within 5 years | Year 4 | Year 3 |

Case 3 earns $0.42M more in Year 5 and $0.27M less over five years, because partner fees, onboarding and staff come
before the study revenue. The extra Year-5 value comes from studies: with no studies sold, Case 3's Year-5 impact is
$2.39M, below Case 2. Buying a bigger partner network doesn't help inside five years either: at 450 partners, Year-5
impact falls to $2.49M because fees and staff grow faster than study and license revenue. Year-5 impact stays above
Case 2 up to a data fee of about $5,200 per partner clinic a year.

Buy partner data when a manufacturer has committed to a drug-safety study, and size the partner network to the
study's gate: about 60–100 clinics.

## What has to be true

| For this to hold | How to check it | When |
|---|---|---|
| Demand forecasting cuts staffing cost ~7% in real locations | Staffing pilot in 2–3 locations against a matched control | First 90 days |
| Systems from acquired brands can be unified at ~$15,000 a location | Integration estimate from IT for the first 10 locations | First 60 days |
| Customers consent to de-identified use | Consent language at registration; legal review | Before any external sale |
| Dental under-charting exists at a similar scale | Run the charting scorecard on real records | First 60 days |
| Reminders retain members | Randomized reminder trial (6,000 households) | Months 3–9 |
| The location count and vet-clinic count | Company records; the public figure is 190+ (January 2024) | First 30 days |
| A manufacturer will pay for a drug-safety study (Case 3) | A signed study before buying partner data | Before any partner contract |
